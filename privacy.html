Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

The Facial Bar Co. LLC ("The Facial Bar," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website located at thefacialbar.co (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the Site, how we use it, and the choices you have — including information collected through text messages (SMS). By using the Site or submitting your information to us, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Who we are

The Facial Bar Co. LLC is an express K-beauty facial bar opening in Orem, Utah. You can reach us using the contact information at the bottom of this page.

2. Information we collect

When you join our waitlist or otherwise fill out a form on the Site, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number, if you choose to provide one
  • Any message or notes you include, and which service or membership you're interested in

We do not currently collect payment or booking information through this Site — booking happens through our scheduling partner once we open.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to your waitlist request and answer questions you send us
  • Email you updates about our opening, availability, and founding-member offers
  • If you provide a phone number, send you text messages about your waitlist status and our opening (see Section 5, SMS Consent, below)
  • Improve the Site and understand interest in our services

4. We do not sell or share your information for marketing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information or mobile information — including your phone number — with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Your information is used only by The Facial Bar Co. LLC to communicate with you as described in this policy.

We do work with a small number of service providers who process information strictly on our behalf, and only so the Site and our booking system can function:

  • Formspree — processes and delivers waitlist form submissions to us. Formspree does not use your information for its own marketing.
  • Boulevard — our booking and scheduling software, which we will use once we open to manage appointments. Boulevard does not use your information for its own marketing.

These providers are bound to use your information only to perform the service we've hired them for. We may also disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid legal request.

5. SMS / text message consent

By entering your phone number on our waitlist form, you are agreeing to receive text messages from The Facial Bar Co. about your waitlist status and our opening. Message & data rates may apply. Message frequency may vary. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase or service.

Reply HELP for more information. You can reply STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE at any time to opt out of text messages. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All other categories, including phone numbers for text messaging opt-in purposes, will not be shared with any third parties.

6. Data retention and security

We keep the information you submit for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to you, keep you updated about our opening, and manage our waitlist, or until you ask us to delete it. We take reasonable measures to protect your information, though no method of transmission or storage is ever completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete your information, unsubscribe from emails, or opt out of text messages at any time (reply STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE, or contact us using the information below).

8. Children's privacy

The Site is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our business grows and opens. We'll update the effective date above when we do. We encourage you to check back periodically.
