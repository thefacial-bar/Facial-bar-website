Our Story

Built by someone who loved facials, not the price tag.

The Facial Bar Co. was founded by Cali McKay, a Utah Valley University-trained counselor who traded spreadsheets of school schedules for spreadsheets of skincare protocols. She loved getting facials but could never quite justify the time or the price tag that came with a real one — so she built The Facial Bar to deliver the same real K-beauty results in 35 minutes, for $35.

She runs The Facial Bar alongside her husband, Sterling McKay, who leads marketing and brand.

Why $35

Affordable. Not cheap.

A traditional facial in Utah runs about $130. Ours is $35 — not because we cut corners, but because we cut the fluff: the upsells, the padding, and the overhead that come with a traditional spa visit. Every protocol still uses real K-beauty technique and real product.

We want every guest to walk out feeling like they just got a $130 facial — because they got the results of one. They just paid $35 for it.
